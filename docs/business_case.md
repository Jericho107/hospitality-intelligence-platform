# Business Case

## Scenario

A synthetic multi-property hospitality group is experiencing a familiar management contradiction:

> Revenue is increasing, but operating contribution is not improving proportionally.

Management has multiple reports, but the reports do not isolate the economic mechanism.

## Hypotheses to test

The platform will be designed to distinguish between at least these causes:

- distribution cost is eroding room-revenue growth;
- ADR improvement is concentrated in a low-volume segment;
- food or beverage cost is worsening;
- supplier price variance is increasing;
- menu mix is shifting toward weak contribution;
- inventory consumption exceeds theoretical consumption;
- labour hours are not flexing with demand;
- one property or outlet is masking another's deterioration;
- forecasts are systematically biased around peak demand.

These are hypotheses, not pre-written findings.

Synthetic data generation must allow some of them to be true and others false.

## Management outputs

A strong final case should enable a GM, CFO, Revenue Manager or F&B Director to answer:

1. Where did margin change?
2. Which driver explains the change?
3. Is the driver commercial or operational?
4. Is it local or systemic?
5. What action is justified?
6. What metric should move if the action works?
7. What could make the conclusion wrong?

## Value model

No ROI figure will be invented.

Any future value estimate must expose:

- baseline;
- intervention;
- unit economics;
- assumptions;
- sensitivity;
- measurement window;
- attribution limitation.

The final case can model potential value using synthetic assumptions, but it must never present modeled value as realized client savings.
