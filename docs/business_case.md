# Business Case

## Management problem

The flagship is built around a specific operating tension:

> Revenue can increase while controllable operating contribution deteriorates.

A management team therefore needs more than a revenue dashboard. It needs to isolate whether performance pressure comes from demand, rate, channel mix, F&B mix, purchasing, waste, discounting, labour or a combination of those drivers.

## Synthetic operating portfolio

The case models three distinct property archetypes:

- urban hotel;
- leisure resort;
- boutique hotel.

This makes cross-property comparison useful without pretending that one operating model generalises to every hospitality business.

## Controlled scenario

The current source generator includes a documented `margin_leakage` scenario.

During the final 20 days of the generated period, the synthetic leisure resort receives controlled pressure in three areas:

- beverage purchase unit cost increases;
- beverage waste increases;
- F&B actual labour and overtime increase.

Demand also strengthens slightly, creating the intended diagnostic tension: topline performance can look healthy while controllable cost pressure worsens.

This scenario is synthetic. It is not a claim about a real property, client or observed commercial result.

## Target management decisions

The final system must support decisions such as:

1. which property or department requires attention first;
2. whether revenue growth is contribution-accretive;
3. whether a variance is driven by rate, volume, mix or controllable cost;
4. which cost driver is large enough to justify intervention;
5. whether staffing and purchasing respond proportionately to demand;
6. whether an action produces measurable improvement after implementation.
