# Limitations

This repository is an end-to-end synthetic hospitality analytics implementation.

- All properties, outlets, bookings, purchases, inventory, labour and budget records are synthetic.
- Diagnostic findings recover controlled injected drivers; they do not establish real-world causal attribution.
- Forecasting results are measured on synthetic demand patterns and are not production hotel-forecast accuracy claims.
- Management opportunity values are scenario estimates under explicit recovery assumptions, not realised savings or client ROI.
- Power BI source assets, TMDL, DAX and PBIR conformance are implemented, but committed Power BI Desktop runtime screenshots and independent rendered-UX review remain pending.
- Production alert thresholds, operational observability, secrets management and support procedures are outside the current implementation.
- Real intervention impact would require observed pre/post action data and an appropriate attribution design.
