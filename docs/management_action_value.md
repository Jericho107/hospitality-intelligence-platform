# Management Action & Value Proof

## Decision objective

The analytical system should not stop at anomaly detection.

For each material operating pressure, management needs:

1. the observed exposure;
2. the action;
3. the owner;
4. the modeled recoverable range;
5. the metric that should move if the action works;
6. the review window;
7. the limitation that could invalidate the estimate.

## Current model

The value model uses the final 20 days as the analysis window and the preceding 20 days as the internal operating baseline.

Scope: the synthetic leisure resort, focused on the controlled F&B leakage scenario.

### Purchase price

Observed exposure:

positive beverage purchase-price variance against governed standard cost.

Action:

reopen supplier pricing and purchase terms.

### Waste

Observed exposure:

waste above the prior-period beverage waste rate, valued at current weighted unit cost.

Action:

tighten portioning, stock handling and waste logging.

### Overtime

Observed exposure:

overtime hours above the prior-period overtime share, valued only at the modeled overtime premium component.

Action:

re-align staffing plans and overtime triggers.

## Recovery scenarios

The model reports three explicit recovery assumptions:

- conservative: 25%;
- base: 50%;
- stretch: 75%.

These are scenarios, not predictions.

## What the number means

A modeled recoverable opportunity is:

observed exposure × recovery assumption.

It is not:

- realized savings;
- client ROI;
- annualized benefit;
- causal proof;
- a guarantee.

## Measurement loop

Each action has a named owner, a governed follow-up metric and a 30-day review window.

The intended consulting loop is:

signal → hypothesis → action → measurement → revised decision.

## Synthetic truth test

CI evaluates the same opportunity model independently on matched healthy and margin-leakage source systems.

The leakage scenario must create materially higher modeled opportunity overall and higher exposure for purchase price, waste and overtime premium.

This is a validation of model responsiveness, not a production claim.
